# R1333 Sponsored Visibility + Rich Member Profile Receipt

Date: 2026-09-18
Product: Franklin Navigator

## Owner decisions implemented
- Active paid Community Members receive materially richer public presentation.
- Paid Community Members are eligible for clearly labeled Sponsored / Community Member visibility above relevant ordinary unpaid profiles and in relevant Franklin directory/search results.
- Sponsored placement communicates increased visibility only. It does not state or imply recommendation, endorsement, superior quality, better reviews, better credentials or better factual accuracy.
- Ordinary unpaid-result ordering remains separate from sponsored placement.
- Profile pages use more visual color hierarchy while preserving Franklin's clean professional design.

## Fresh benchmark adaptation
Current Avvo advertising materials state that sponsored listings position advertisers at the top of directory pages when consumers search for attorneys, and current Yelp advertising materials describe sponsored results above/below relevant search results and placements on pages of similar businesses. Franklin adapts the visibility pattern without copying either site's visual design or claims.

## Paid public-profile upgrades
- Larger photo-first cover/gallery mosaic when approved member media exists.
- Community Member badge.
- Member tagline near identity.
- Member At-a-glance card with hours, service area, languages, pricing and primary conversion links when supplied and approved.
- Existing reviewed member sections remain: about, services, practical details, credentials/experience, events/updates, gallery, online presence.
- Dedicated Franklin Navigator special-offer module remains active.
- Paid member profile suppresses ordinary competitor/related-profile card under the existing member rule.

## Sponsored visibility
- Unpaid commercial/professional profile pages can display relevant active paid members immediately below the profile hero in a clearly labeled Sponsored / Franklin Community Members module.
- Franklin directory/search can display up to two relevant sponsored Community Members above ordinary matching results on page one.
- Directory sponsored candidates must also match the user's active ordinary filters/search because client-side relevance is evaluated against the same Franklin directory record set.
- Backend sponsor feed only admits active accounts with verified profile authority, active rich-profile entitlement and an active membership lifecycle.
- Sponsored inventory rotates deterministically by day/context rather than creating a quality ranking.
- Government, civic and nonprofit profile pages do not receive the commercial sponsored-profile module.

## Color/presentation improvement
- Main website CTA remains Franklin teal.
- Call action uses a restrained green treatment.
- Email/contact actions use restrained blue treatment.
- Directions uses a warm gold treatment.
- Profile fact panels rotate subtle teal/blue/gold/violet tints.
- Reviews use a restrained blue-tinted panel.
- Special offers use the existing warm offer treatment.
- Sponsored placements use a subtle gold advertising treatment.

## Integrity boundaries
- Paid status does not change factual accuracy.
- Paid status does not change review score or review moderation.
- Paid status does not reorder the ordinary unpaid-result list.
- No sponsored placement is labeled recommended, endorsed, best, top-rated or preferred.
- No fake paid member, fake review, fake offer or fake gallery was created for proof.

## Production status
- Runtime sponsored-placement API is deployed from franklin-commerce-runtime-r30.
- Public site rich-member/sponsored-profile and directory layers are deployed from main.
- First genuine public paid-member sponsored placement remains dependent on a real eligible public Community Member; control/test memberships are not production evidence.
