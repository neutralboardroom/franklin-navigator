Recorded: 2026-09-19

Release: FR-NAV1.30.41-HF3.13.23

## Owner direction
Make it clearer to potential paying members that Community Membership can increase their visibility and explain how it works for them.

## Public value model
Community Membership does not sell factual accuracy, endorsement, undisclosed organic ranking, or guaranteed leads.

The public explanation now makes the visibility mechanism concrete:

1. **Richer profile content** — reviewed About, services, hours, accessibility, photos and other practical details give residents more useful reasons to notice and understand the business.
2. **More action paths** — verified website, phone, email, booking, quote, order/menu and other eligible links help residents take the next step once they find the profile.
3. **Clearly labeled paid visibility** — active members may be eligible for clearly labeled Community Member / Sponsored placement in relevant Franklin discovery areas, separate from ordinary unpaid ranking.
4. **Timely member content** — reviewed special offers and other eligible Community Member features can give residents additional useful reasons to engage.
5. **Focused member profile** — active member profiles do not show the Similar local profiles section.

## Trust boundary
- Basic factual public profiles remain useful without payment.
- Factual corrections and removal requests remain free.
- Payment does not change canonical profile facts.
- Ordinary unpaid ranking remains independent of membership.
- Sponsored/member placements must be clearly labeled.
- Membership does not guarantee leads, customers, placement, availability, endorsement or results.
